Before you begin

A few things to know.

Does the widget show the event's name, place, or start time?

The widget shown here displays time remaining. Open the app to check your plans and event details.

Does it calculate when I should leave?

It shows time until the scheduled event. Decide separately how much time you need to get ready and travel.

Which event does the widget use?

It looks for the next eligible event today, then tomorrow if there is none today. This page does not promise a countdown to any date far in the future.

Will the trial ending charge me?

No. The trial ending does not automatically charge you. Free use continues with limits unless you choose Pro.
